3.1 DISEASE PREDICTION ALGORITHMS
----------------------------------

a) Obesity Prediction (predict_obesity)
   Input: age, gender, BMI
   Model: DecisionTreeClassifier/RandomForestClassifier
   Output: Obesity class (Underweight, Normal Weight, Overweight, Obese Class I/II/III)
   Features: Age, encoded gender, BMI
   Custom mapping: 7-class obesity classification system

b) Diabetes Prediction (predict_diabetes)
   Input: age, gender, BMI, HbA1c, blood glucose
   Model: Disease prediction model (RandomForest)
   Output: Diabetes status
   Features: Age, encoded gender, BMI, HbA1c, blood glucose levels
   Encoding: Label encoder for disease classification

c) Kidney Disease Prediction (predict_kidney)
   Input: age, gender, BMI, sodium, potassium, blood pressure, creatinine
   Model: Kidney disease prediction model
   Output: Kidney disease status
   Features: Age, encoded gender, BMI, sodium, potassium, BP, serum creatinine
   Encoding: Label encoder with whitespace stripping

3.2 NUTRITION RECOMMENDATION ALGORITHM
---------------------------------------

Class: IntelligentNutritionRecommender

a) Disease Condition Detection
   - Parses disease list to identify: Diabetes, Kidney Disease, Obesity
   - Handles multiple concurrent diseases
   - Creates condition flags for filtering

b) Food Filtering System
   Diabetes filters:
   - Free Sugar ≤ 8g
   - Calories ≤ 400 kcal
   
   Kidney Disease filters:
   - Sodium ≤ 150mg
   - Protein ≤ 15g
   
   Obesity filters:
   - Calories ≤ 350 kcal
   - Fats ≤ 15g
   
   Normal/Balanced filters:
   - Calories ≤ 500 kcal
   - Free Sugar ≤ 15g

c) Nutritional Scoring Algorithm
   Base score: Fiber × 2
   
   Diabetes adjustments:
   - Sugar penalty: -3 per gram
   - Carb penalty: -0.5 per gram
   - Fiber bonus: +3 per gram
   
   Kidney Disease adjustments:
   - Sodium penalty: -0.1 per mg
   - Protein penalty: -0.5 per gram
   
   Obesity adjustments:
   - Calorie penalty: -0.05 per kcal
   - Fat penalty: -0.5 per gram
   - Protein bonus: +1.5 per gram
   - Fiber bonus: +2 per gram
   
   Normal adjustments:
   - Protein bonus: +1 per gram
   - Fiber bonus: +1.5 per gram
   - Sugar penalty: -1 per gram

d) Foods to Avoid Algorithm
   Diabetes: High sugar foods (>15g)
   Kidney Disease: High sodium (>400mg), high protein (>20g)
   Obesity: High calorie foods (>500kcal)
   Normal: Extreme sugar/sodium items

3.3 HEALTH CALCULATION ALGORITHMS
----------------------------------

a) BMI Calculation
   Formula: weight (kg) / (height (m))²
   Categories: Underweight (<18.5), Normal (18.5-24.9), Overweight (25-29.9), Obese (≥30)

b) BMR Calculation (Mifflin-St Jeor Equation)
   Male: 10 × weight + 6.25 × height - 5 × age + 5
   Female: 10 × weight + 6.25 × height - 5 × age - 161

c) TDEE Calculation
   Formula: BMR × Activity Factor
   Activity Factors:
   - Sedentary: 1.2
   - Light: 1.375
   - Moderate: 1.55
   - Active: 1.725
   - Very Active: 1.9

d) Water Intake Calculation
   Base: 35ml per kg body weight
   Activity multipliers: 1.0 (Sedentary) to 1.4 (Very Active)

e) Protein Requirement Calculation
   Kidney Disease: 0.6g/kg (restricted)
   Obesity: 1.2g/kg (high for satiety)
   Diabetes: 1.0g/kg (moderate)
   Normal: 0.8g/kg (standard)
   Age adjustment: +0.1g/kg for >65 years
   Gender adjustment: +0.1g/kg for males
